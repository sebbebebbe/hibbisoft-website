# Language flag icons

The Sweden (`se.svg`), United Kingdom (`gb.svg`), Netherlands (`nl.svg`) and Germany (`de.svg`) icons are from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), using its 4:3 SVG variants. They are served locally and retain their original artwork. The MIT license is included in `LICENSE`.

These are decorative visual cues beside the written language names Swedish, English, Dutch and German. The language names remain visible and accessible; flags do not indicate citizenship, nationality or proficiency level.
